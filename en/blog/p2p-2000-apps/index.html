P2P store: 2000+ apps

March 20, 2025 · Alexsey_Pip · Ecosystem

The FuturaOS decentralized app catalog has reached 2000 packages. This is a major milestone for our P2P ecosystem.

New categories

New sections added: games, multimedia, development tools, office apps, and network utilities. Popular packages: FuturaWriter, PeerChat, V8 Studio, MeshVPN, IPFS Desktop.

How it works

Every FuturaOS user is a network node. When you install an app, you also help distribute it to other users. No central servers — only IPFS and DHT.
